Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Bridge free?

Yes. You can convert 2 files every week for free, with files up to 10 MB (25 MB to compress). Reading and editing PDF and Word files is always free. Pro removes the weekly limit and adds signing, Autofill, searchable scans and size limits.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, email or password. Open the app and start.

What happens to my files?

Files sent for converting travel over an encrypted connection and are deleted as soon as the result reaches your phone, or after about an hour at most. Finished files are saved only on your phone, never in a cloud account.

Can it convert bank statements to Excel?

Yes. Statements become spreadsheets with real numbers and dates, and every running balance is checked so you can see straight away whether the sheet adds up.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Many bank statements and official documents are locked. Type the password when you convert; it is used only to open that file.

Which languages can it read in scans and photos?

34, including English, Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian and Malay.

Which phones does it work on?

The Android and iPhone apps are coming soon. Until then, you can convert one file free in your browser on a computer, from any tool page.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, and PDF Bridge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.
