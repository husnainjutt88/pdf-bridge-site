The problem it solves

Why use PDF Bridge to turn a photo into editable text

Retyping a printed page, a handout or a screenshot is slow and full of mistakes. Optical character recognition (OCR) reads the text for you, but simple OCR apps give you one long block of words.

PDF Bridge straightens and cleans each photo first, reads the text in the language you choose, and keeps the shape of the page: headings, paragraphs and tables stay in order. The result is a Word document you can edit in the app or share.

Step by step

How to turn a photo into editable text

  1. Take or pick photos

    Use the camera, or choose photos and screenshots you already have. Several pages make one document.

  2. Choose the language

    Pick the language of the text, from English, Arabic and Urdu to Chinese, Japanese and Hindi.

  3. Edit the Word file

    Fix anything you like in the app, then share the .docx or turn it into a PDF.

Questions

Photo to Word (OCR): frequently asked questions

Which languages can it read?

34 languages: Arabic, Bengali, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu and Vietnamese.

Can it read handwriting?

It is made for printed and typed text. Very neat handwriting may be read, but results vary, so check the text afterwards.

How do I get the best result?

Photograph the page flat, in good light, filling most of the picture. The app straightens and cleans the page, but sharp photos read best.

Can I convert several photos into one document?

Yes. Pick up to 3 photos at once for free, or up to 20 with Pro, and they become one Word document in order.

Is it free?

On a computer you can convert one file free right here in your browser, with no account. The Android and iPhone apps, coming soon, give 2 free files every week, and Pro removes the limit.
