The problem it solves

Why use PDF Bridge to convert PDF to Excel

A PDF table looks tidy until you try to use the numbers. Copy and paste brings the columns across as one long line, and many converters give you cells full of text that won't add up.

PDF Bridge finds the tables on each page and rebuilds them as a real spreadsheet. Numbers, currencies and dates are stored as values, merged headings stay readable, and every table keeps its own rows and columns. Open the result in the app, or share the .xlsx file to Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

Questions

PDF to Excel: frequently asked questions

Will the formatting of my table be kept?

The structure is kept: rows, columns, headings and the order of the tables. The spreadsheet is built to be used, so values are clean numbers and dates rather than copies of the PDF's fonts and colours.

Can it convert a scanned PDF to Excel?

Yes. Scanned pages are read with OCR in 34 languages, including English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Chinese and Spanish, then laid out as a table.

What file do I get?

An .xlsx file that opens in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers and other spreadsheet apps. It is saved on your phone.

Is there a page limit?

Free files can be up to 10 MB and Pro files up to 50 MB. A single PDF can have up to 200 pages.

Is it free?

Yes, for 2 files every week. Pro gives unlimited conversions, larger files and editing of cells and rows in the app.
