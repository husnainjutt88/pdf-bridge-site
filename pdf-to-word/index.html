The problem it solves

Why use PDF Bridge to convert PDF to Word

Most PDF to Word converters give you a document that technically contains the text but looks nothing like the PDF: tables collapse, columns run into each other and pictures end up on the wrong page.

PDF Bridge rebuilds each page so the Word file matches the PDF. Choose "Keep the layout" for forms, letters and reports that must look the same, or "Flowing text" when you want plain paragraphs that are easiest to rewrite. Then edit the .docx right in the app, or open it in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

Questions

PDF to Word: frequently asked questions

Will my PDF look the same in Word?

With "Keep the layout", the Word document is built to match the PDF page by page: tables, columns, images and backgrounds stay where they were. Very complex designs may need small touches, which you can make in the app.

Can I edit the Word file on my phone?

Yes. The built-in editor changes text, font size, colour and highlight, adds headings, lists, tables and page breaks, and finds and replaces words. Anything you don't touch stays exactly as it was.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned pages are read with OCR in 34 languages and turned into editable text.

What about password-protected PDFs?

Type the password when you convert. It is used only to open that file.

Is PDF to Word free?

You can convert 2 files a week for free, up to 10 MB each. Reading and editing Word files in the app is always free.
