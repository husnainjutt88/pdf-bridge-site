The problem it solves

Why use PDF Bridge to scan documents to PDF

A photo of a document is not a scan: the page is tilted, the paper looks grey and the file is too big to send. Forms, receipts and contracts need a proper PDF.

PDF Bridge finds the edges of each page, flattens it, turns it upright and cleans it so the paper is white and the text is sharp. Several pages become one PDF in the order you choose. Already have the pictures? Photos to PDF turns JPG and PNG images from your gallery into a PDF the same way.

Step by step

How to scan documents to PDF on your phone

  1. Scan or pick photos

    Point the camera at each page, or choose JPG and PNG pictures from your gallery.

  2. Choose the look

    Document gives white paper and sharp black text; Colour keeps stamps, photos and highlights.

  3. Save the PDF

    One PDF on your phone, ready to email, upload or print.

Questions

Scan to PDF: frequently asked questions

How many pages can I scan into one PDF?

Up to 3 pages a job on the free plan and up to 20 with Pro. Each scan is one PDF in the order you took the pictures.

Can I turn photos from my gallery into a PDF?

Yes. Choose Photos to PDF and pick JPG or PNG images; they are cleaned up and joined into one PDF.

What is a searchable PDF?

A PDF with the text read and stored behind the picture, so you can search for words and copy them. Searchable scans are part of Pro.

Is the scanner free?

Yes, it is one of your 2 free files each week. Pro removes the weekly limit.
