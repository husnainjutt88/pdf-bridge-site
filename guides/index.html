Guides

Practical answers to the problems people bring to PDF Bridge.

These guides answer the questions people bring to PDF Bridge most often: why a bank statement lands in one column in Excel, how to get older statements into a spreadsheet, what to send with a visa or home loan application, how to fit an ID card's front and back on one page, and how to get a PDF under an upload limit.

Each guide explains the problem first and gives steps that work with any tool, then links to the PDF Bridge tool that does it for you. Where rules differ by bank or country, the guides say so and point to the official source.
