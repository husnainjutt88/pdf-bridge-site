Getting older or PDF-only bank statements into Excel

Bank exports usually cover recent transactions only. Here is what to do with everything else.

Updated 26 November 2026

Many banks let you export transactions as CSV, but only for a limited window, and the official monthly statements are often PDF only. If you need a year of statements for a mortgage, a tax return or bookkeeping, you usually end up with PDFs.

What some banks offer themselves

From the banks' own help pages, checked in November 2026. Banks change these options, so check your bank's help pages for the current details.

BankWhat its help pages describe
HSBC UKTransactions can be downloaded from the transaction list; statements themselves are PDFs. (HSBC help)
NatWestTransactions can be exported from online banking, including to Excel. (NatWest help)
MonzoTransactions can be exported from the app as CSV, QIF or PDF. (Monzo help)
FNB (South Africa)Transaction search results can be downloaded as CSV for a limited period. (FNB guide, PDF)

For other banks, look for Export, Download, "Spreadsheet" or "CSV" on the transactions page. If you can't find one, or the dates you need are outside the window, the PDF is your source.

When the PDF is all you have

  • Statements older than the export window: download the monthly PDFs from the statements section and convert them.
  • Statements someone sent you (a client, tenant or applicant): you can't log in to their bank, so convert the PDF.
  • Closed accounts: online banking access often ends, but you may still have the PDFs.
  • Paper statements: photograph or scan each page flat and well lit, then convert; the text is read with OCR.
  • Password-protected PDFs: many banks lock statements with a date of birth or part of the account number. A converter that asks for the password opens it for that conversion only.

Putting a year together

  1. Convert each month's statement to Excel.
  2. Check each one: the opening balance should equal the previous month's closing balance.
  3. Copy the transactions into one sheet, keeping one header row, and sort by date.
