Delete your PDF Bridge data

How to delete the data the PDF Bridge app keeps about you, what is deleted and what is kept.

PDF Bridge has no accounts: there is no sign-up, email or password, and your files are saved only on your phone. This page covers the PDF Bridge app for Android and iPhone.

How to delete your data

  1. Delete the files on your phone. In PDF Bridge, open the Files tab, then delete any file (tap ⋯ › Delete). To remove everything at once, uninstall the app: that deletes all your files, your saved details (My details) and your signature from the phone.
  2. Ask us to delete what we hold about your device. In the app, open Account › Support. An email to support@pdfbridge.app opens with your device reference already filled in. Write "Please delete my data" and send it. No app? Email support@pdfbridge.app from any address and we'll tell you how to find your device reference.
  3. Get our confirmation. We delete the records within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Files and photos you converted, and form answers. Already deleted automatically: they are removed from our server as soon as the result reaches your phone, or after about one hour at most. Nothing to ask for.
  • Usage events and crash reports. Deleted on request. Otherwise kept, with the device ID in hashed form, for 180 days.
  • Your free-file count and device record. Deleted on request.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. If you subscribed to Pro, Google Play and our payment provider RevenueCat keep the record of the purchase for as long as billing, tax and refund rules require. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments and subscriptions.
  • Advertising data held by Google. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which keeps data under Google's own policy. You can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy. Questions: support@pdfbridge.app.
