About PDF Bridge

Converters that get real documents right, with files deleted after converting.

What PDF Bridge is

PDF Bridge turns PDFs, scans and photos into documents you can work with: Word files that look like the original, Excel sheets with real numbers, bank statements with every running balance checked, filled-in forms, ID card copies and smaller PDFs.

It started from a simple frustration: most converters produce something that technically contains the text but needs an hour of cleanup. PDF Bridge is built around the documents people actually struggle with: bank statements, government forms, scanned pages, and files in languages like Arabic, Urdu and Hindi.

Where you can use it

  • On a computer: every tool page lets you convert one file free in your browser.
  • On your phone: the Android and iPhone apps are coming soon. Get an email when they're out.

How your files are handled

  • Files go to our server over an encrypted connection only to make the result, and are deleted as soon as it's ready, or after about an hour at most.
  • We don't keep copies and don't use your files to train anything.
  • No account is needed. The details are in our privacy policy.

Contact

Questions, problems, or a document that didn't convert well: support@pdfbridge.app. Every message is read.
