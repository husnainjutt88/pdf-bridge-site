The problem it solves

Why use PDF Bridge to fill in and sign a PDF form

Printing a form, filling it by hand and scanning it back is a chore, and most phone apps can only fill PDFs that already have form fields. Many real forms, especially scanned ones, have none.

PDF Bridge finds the boxes, lines and tick boxes on most forms, including flat and scanned ones, and gives you a simple list to fill in. Your answers are placed neatly on the page. With Pro, save your details once to fill a form in one tap, and sign with your own signature.

Questions

Fill & sign PDF forms: frequently asked questions

Can I fill a PDF form that isn't fillable?

Yes. On most flat and scanned forms, the app finds the boxes and lines to write in, so you can fill them like a fillable form.

How do I sign a PDF on my phone?

Draw your signature once and place it on the form or any PDF. Signing is part of Pro.

What does Autofill do?

Save your details once (name, date of birth, address, phone and more) and Autofill puts them in the right places on new forms. Your details are saved only on your phone. Autofill is part of Pro.

Can I fill forms for free?

On a computer you can convert one file free right here in your browser, with no account. The Android and iPhone apps, coming soon, give 2 free files every week, and Pro removes the limit. Signing and Autofill are part of Pro in the app.

Can I print the filled form?

Yes. Save the filled PDF, then print or share it from your phone.
