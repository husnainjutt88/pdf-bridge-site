Privacy Policy

PDF Bridge · Last updated: 17 November 2026

PDF Bridge converts, edits, scans and compresses documents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. We keep it short and plain. If anything here is unclear, contact us (see the bottom of this page).

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • Your files are uploaded only to convert them, and they are deleted as soon as the result is ready, or after about one hour at most.
  • Finished files are saved on your phone. Editing PDFs and Word files happens on your phone, and those edits never leave it.
  • Free users see ads. Pro users see no ads.

2. What we collect

Files you convert. When you convert a file, it is sent to our server over an encrypted connection. We use it only to produce the result. It is deleted as soon as the result is ready, or after about one hour at most. We do not keep copies, and we do not use your files to train anything.

A device identifier. The app uses an identifier for your phone to count free conversions each week, and to link your Pro subscription to your device. The identifier stays the same if you reinstall the app on the same phone.

Usage events. The app records basic events, such as which tool you opened, whether a conversion succeeded, and when you reached the paywall. The device identifier is stored in hashed (scrambled) form and kept for 180 days.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), with RevenueCat managing them for us. We receive only the information needed to know whether you are subscribed. We never see your payment card details.

Ads (free version only). Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your phone's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. On iPhone, this happens only if you allow tracking in the prompt Apple shows you. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't require sign-up, a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't read the contents of your files, beyond what is needed to convert them.
  • We don't use other trackers.

4. Where the data goes

Our conversion server is hosted by HOSTING PROVIDER AND COUNTRY. Google AdMob, Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat process data under their own privacy policies, which are linked below.

5. How long we keep things

  • Uploaded files: deleted once the result is ready, or after about one hour at most.
  • Hashed usage events: 180 days.
  • Files on your phone: until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

6. Your choices

  • Delete any file in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone.
  • Turn off ad tracking in your phone's settings.
  • Contact us to ask what we hold about your device, or to request deletion.

7. Children

PDF Bridge is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
