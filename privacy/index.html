Privacy Policy

PDF Bridge · Last updated: 20 November 2026

PDF Bridge converts, edits, scans and compresses documents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. We keep it short and plain. If anything here is unclear, contact us (see the bottom of this page).

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • Files are sent to our server only for the features that need it (listed below), over an encrypted connection, and deleted as soon as the result reaches your phone, or after about one hour at most.
  • Finished files are saved on your phone. Drawing, notes and signatures in PDFs, and editing Word documents, happen on your phone.
  • Free users see ads. Pro users see no ads.

2. What we collect

Files you send to our server. These features send your file to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS): converting (for example PDF to Word or Excel, photos to Word, scanning to a searchable PDF), compressing, splitting and merging, ID card copies, opening a Word file in its exact view, changing a PDF's own text (Pro), finding and filling form fields, and viewing or editing spreadsheets. We use the file only to produce the result. It is deleted as soon as the result reaches your phone, or after about one hour at most. We do not keep copies, and we do not use your files to train anything.

What you type into forms. When you fill in a form, the answers you type (which can include your name, date of birth, address or ID numbers) are sent with the form to produce the filled PDF, and deleted the same way. Details you save with "My details" are stored only on your phone.

A PDF's password. If a PDF is locked and you type its password, the password is sent with that file only to open it for the conversion. It is kept in the same job folder, deleted with the file, and never written to logs.

A device identifier. The app uses an identifier for your phone to count free conversions each week, and to link your Pro subscription to your device. The identifier stays the same if you reinstall the app on the same phone.

Usage events. The app records basic events, such as which tool you opened, whether a conversion succeeded, and when you reached the paywall, plus your phone's region setting (for example "PK"), so we know which countries the app is used in without using your location. The device identifier is stored in hashed (scrambled) form and kept for 180 days.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), with RevenueCat managing them for us. We receive only the information needed to know whether you are subscribed. We never see your payment card details.

App settings. The app asks Google Firebase which server address to use. Firebase receives a random installation identifier for this, not your files.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it records the type of error, a short code that identifies the bug, the screen and the app version. Never the error message or anything from your files.

Ads (free version only). Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your phone's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. On iPhone, this happens only if you allow tracking in the prompt Apple shows you. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your answer in the app under Account › Ad privacy choices.

Notifications. If you start a free trial, the app can remind you the day before it ends. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; nothing about it is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't require sign-up, a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't read the contents of your files, beyond what is needed to convert them.
  • We don't use any other trackers or analytics services.

4. Where the data goes

Our conversion server is hosted by Contabo GmbH (Munich, Germany), in its European Union data centres. Google (AdMob and Firebase), Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat process data under their own privacy policies, which are linked below.

International transfers. These services, and our server, may be in a different country from yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it (for example for people in the EU or UK), transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep things

  • Files sent to our server, and form answers: deleted as soon as the result reaches your phone, or after about one hour at most.
  • Hashed usage events: 180 days.
  • Files on your phone: until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete any file in the app at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone.
  • Turn off ad tracking in your phone's settings, or limit ads to non-personalised ones under Account › Ad privacy choices (where offered). Pro has no ads.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of ad data (as some US state laws call it): use the same settings. We don't sell your information for money.
  • Ask what we hold about your device, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it: write to us from Account › Support in the app, and we'll tell you what we need to find your device's records. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you are in the EU, the UK or another place with a data protection authority, you can also complain to it.

The legal bases we rely on (for people in the EU and UK): providing the service you asked for (files, purchases), our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (usage events, crash reports, fraud prevention), and your consent for personalised ads.

7. Children

PDF Bridge is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
