Why use PDF Bridge to make an ID card copy

Banks, landlords, employers and offices often ask for a copy of your ID with both sides on one page. Without a photocopier, getting the size and position right is fiddly.

Questions

ID card copy: frequently asked questions

Is the ID copy printed at real size?

Yes. Cards are placed at their true size on an A4 or Letter page, so the printout matches a photocopy.

Does it work with passports?

Yes. Choose passport page, and the photo page is placed on the sheet at real size.

Are photos of my ID kept?

No. The photos are sent over an encrypted connection to make the copy and deleted as soon as the PDF is ready, or after about an hour at most.

Is this a certified copy?

No. It is a clear copy at real size. If a bank or office asks for a certified copy (for example for FICA in South Africa), print it and take it with your original ID to be stamped and signed, for example by a commissioner of oaths.

Does it work with a Smart ID, CNIC or driving licence?

Yes. South African Smart ID cards, Pakistani CNICs, most national ID cards and driving licences are bank-card size, and both sides are placed on one page at that size.

Can I upload the ID copy PDF to an online form?

Yes. The PDF is small enough for most upload forms. If a form has a strict size limit, see our guide to making a PDF small enough for an online form.
