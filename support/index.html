Common questions

My purchase went through but Pro isn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If Pro still doesn't appear, email us and include the error code shown in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PDF Bridge.
On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → PDF Bridge.
Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

My conversion failed or the file is too big

Check you're connected to the internet. Free files are limited to 10 MB and Pro to 50 MB. A PDF with more than 200 pages can't be converted. If the problem keeps happening, email us the file type and size (don't send the file itself unless we ask).

I used all my free conversions

Free allows a few conversions each week. The limit resets weekly, or you can subscribe to Pro for unlimited conversions.

Where are my converted files?

They're saved on your phone. Open the Files tab in the app.

How do I delete my data?

Delete files in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything on your phone. For questions about data we hold, see our Privacy Policy.

Something else?

Email us at the address above. Include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened.
