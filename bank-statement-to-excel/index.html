Why use PDF Bridge to convert a bank statement PDF to Excel

Copying a bank statement into a spreadsheet by hand takes an hour and one wrong digit spoils the totals. Most PDF converters make it worse: amounts arrive as text, descriptions that run over two lines split into extra rows, and the balance column no longer matches.

PDF Bridge reads the statement the way an accountant would. It keeps each transaction on one row, turns dates and amounts into real Excel values you can sort and add up, and then checks every running balance against the one before it. If a row doesn't add up, it is highlighted and listed on a separate Checks sheet, so you know exactly which line to look at.

Bank statement to Excel: frequently asked questions

Which banks does it work with?

It doesn't rely on templates for particular banks: it reads the table on the page, so it works with statements from most banks and card issuers. Because every balance is checked, you can see straight away whether a statement came out right.

Can it open a password-protected bank statement?

Yes. When a statement is locked, the app asks for the password, which is often your date of birth or part of your account number. It is used only to open that file and is not saved.

Will the amounts be numbers in Excel?

Yes. Amounts and dates are stored as real Excel numbers and dates, not text, so SUM, sorting and filters work without any clean-up.

What does the balance check do?

For each transaction, the app works out what the balance should be from the previous balance and the amount, and compares it with the balance printed on the statement. Matching rows are counted; rows that differ are highlighted and listed on a Checks sheet.

Can I convert a scanned or photographed statement?

Yes. Scans and phone photos are read with OCR, then laid out and checked like any other statement. A clear, flat, well-lit photo gives the best result.

Is it free?

On a computer you can convert one file free right here in your browser, with no account. The Android and iPhone apps, coming soon, give 2 free files every week, and Pro removes the limit.

Is my bank statement kept on your server?

No. The file is sent over an encrypted connection to be converted and deleted as soon as the result is ready for you, or after about an hour at most. We never keep a copy.
