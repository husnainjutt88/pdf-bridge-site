Why use PDF Bridge to compress a PDF to 100 KB

Government portals, job sites and university applications often refuse any PDF over 100 KB or 200 KB, and a scanned page from a phone can easily be 2 MB.

Questions

Compress PDF: frequently asked questions

How do I compress a PDF to 100 KB?

Open the PDF, choose Compress PDF, pick Size limit and choose 100 KB (or type another size). The app makes the best-quality file that fits under it. Size limits are part of Pro.

Will compressing make my PDF blurry?

Pictures are reduced only as much as needed to reach the size, and text is kept sharp. Very large scans squeezed to a tiny limit may be saved in black and white so the text stays clear; the app tells you when it does.

What if my PDF can't get small enough?

The app tells you, and shows the smallest size it could reach, so you can split the file or remove pages.

Is compressing free?

In your browser you can compress one PDF free (up to 10 MB). In the apps, coming soon, balanced compression is free for files up to 25 MB, and the Smallest setting and exact size limits like 100 KB are part of Pro.

Can I also merge or split PDFs?

Yes. Merge PDFs joins files in the order you choose, and Split PDF takes out the pages you need or cuts a PDF into separate files.
